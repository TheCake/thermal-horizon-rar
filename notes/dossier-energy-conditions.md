# Dossier: energy conditions & quantum bounds on negative energy (research agent output, 2026-07-20)

- Pointwise conditions NEC/WEC/SEC/DEC all violated by QFT (Casimir, squeezed vacuum). The survivor is **achronal ANEC** — now essentially a theorem in flat-space QFT:
  - Faulkner et al., JHEP 09 (2016) 038 (from monotonicity of relative entropy)
  - Hartman, Kundu, Tajdini, JHEP 07 (2017) 066 (from causality + unitarity)
  - Graham & Olum, PRD 76, 064001 (2007): self-consistent achronal ANEC rules out traversable wormholes and CTCs.
  - QNEC proven (Balakrishnan et al., arXiv:1706.09432) → implies ANEC.
- Casimir (verified): ρ = −π²ħc/720a⁴; a=10 nm → −4.33×10⁴ J/m³ (−4.8×10⁻¹³ kg/m³); measured to ~1% (Mohideen & Roy, PRL 81, 4549).
- Ford–Roman QI (verified coefficient): ρ̂ ≥ −3/(32π²t₀⁴) (massless scalar, Lorentzian sampling; PRD 51, 4277). Translation: |ΔE|·Δt ≲ ħ. At t₀ = 1 s: ρ ≥ −3.7×10⁻⁶² J/m³ → accumulating −1 J needs a ~10-kpc-scale region.
- Quantum interest conjecture (Ford & Roman, PRD 60, 104018): every negative pulse must be overcompensated by positive energy, with interest growing with separation; proven in key cases (Abreu & Visser, PRD 79, 065004).
- Pfenning & Ford, CQG 14, 1743 (1997): Alcubierre bubble wall restricted to ~10² v_b Planck lengths thick; total energy for R=100 m bubble: **E ≲ −6.2×10⁶² v_b kg** (~10 orders of magnitude more than the visible universe's mass). If QIs could be evaded and wall ~1 m: still ~ −¼ solar mass of negative energy.
- Warp status: Santiago, Schuster & Visser (PRD 105, 064038): **generic warp drives violate the NEC** — including all "positive energy" claims (Lentz etc.), which used only Eulerian observers. Bobrick & Martire 2021: classification; subluminal shells still need exotic components for usable configurations. Newest: arXiv:2512.18008 (Dec 2025) irrotational-shift solution reduces energy deficit ~38× vs Alcubierre — still NEC-violating.
- QEIs proven rigorously for free fields on curved spacetimes (Fewster, arXiv:1208.5399) and 2D CFTs; no general interacting-4D QEI, but ANEC/QNEC hold.
