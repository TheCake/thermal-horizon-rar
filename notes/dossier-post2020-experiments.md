# Dossier: post-2020 gravity/superconductor experiments (research agent output, 2026-07-20)

## Direct SC-gravity searches — all null
- Tajmar, Neunzig & Kößling, Front. Phys. 10, 892215 (2022): Cooper-pair currents up to 15 A in BSCCO/YBCO, 57–77 K. **Null at ~100 nN**; thrust-to-current ≤ 5×10⁻⁹ N/A — 9 orders below Poher-claim (~1 N/A).
- Tajmar, Kößling & Neunzig, Sci. Rep. 14, 19427 (2024) / arXiv:2402.15640: broad search for gravity-EM coupling (asymmetric capacitors, shielded toroids, tunneling currents...). **Null at ~nN / nN·m.**
- Tajmar et al., CEAS Space J. (2021): EmDrive refuted by ≥3 orders of magnitude (NASA Eagleworks signal = thermal drift); Mach-effect thruster = vibration artifact.
- Navy/NAWCAD "Pais effect" HEEMFG test 2016–2019: $508k, "could not be proven"; patents abandoned ~2021.
- No post-2020 atom-interferometer or torsion-balance search near superconductors exists; Ummarino–Gallerati's predicted ~≤10⁻⁷ local-field alteration near Tc remains untested.

## Funded conventional precision experiments (no results yet)
- **Archimedes** (INFN, Sos Enattos mine, Sardinia): cryogenic beam balance to weigh Casimir vacuum-energy change in high-Tc superconductor stacks crossing Tc. Construction completed Mar 2025; calibration underway; target ~10⁻¹⁶–10⁻¹⁵ N-class weight modulation at mHz (verify exact target). arXiv:1409.6974, arXiv:2202.06328.
- **GINGER** (LNGS ring lasers): terrestrial Lense-Thirring to ~0.1% of GR; GINGERINO noise floor ~2×10⁻¹⁵ rad/s; under construction from late 2024. arXiv:2306.15603.
- **Westphal et al., Nature 591, 225 (2021)**: gravity of 90 mg gold spheres measured; accuracy 3×10⁻¹¹ m/s².
- **Fuchs et al., Sci. Adv. 10, eadk2949 (2024)**: 0.43 mg particle in superconducting levitation trap at mK; measured ~3×10⁻¹⁷ N gravitational signal; force noise 0.5 fN/√Hz.

## Ummarino & Gallerati theory line (all theoretical, effects tiny)
- CQG 37, 217001 (2020); Entropy 23, 193 (2021); arXiv:2110.07335; Symmetry 14, 554 (2022) review; Front. Phys. 10, 941858 (2022) with Modanese — conclusion: no confirmed anomalous SC-gravity coupling; predicted back-reactions tiny; dedicated experiments don't exist yet.

**Bottom line:** no post-2020 experiment confirms any anomalous superconductor-gravity coupling; every direct search is null at the nano-newton level.
