# Dossier: gravitomagnetism & negative mass (research agent output, 2026-07-20)

## GEM essentials
- Mashhoon GEM equations (arXiv:gr-qc/0311030): Lorentz-like force F = −mE − 2m(v/c)×B; factor 2 = spin-2 signature. Rotating body: A = (G/c)(J×x)/r³; Lense-Thirring Ω = (G/c²r³)[3(J·r̂)r̂ − J].
- Gravity Probe B (PRL 106, 221101; arXiv:1105.3456): geodetic −6601.8 ± 18.3 mas/yr (GR: −6606.1); frame dragging −37.2 ± 7.2 mas/yr (GR: −39.2) → 19% measurement. Earth's surface B_g ~ 1.7×10⁻¹⁴ s⁻¹.
- LAGEOS/LARES (Ciufolini EPJC 79:872, 2019): μ = 0.991 ± 0.02 (2% test; systematics contested by Iorio). LARES-2 (2022) targets 0.2%, contested (arXiv:2503.07264).
- Lab scale: G/c² = 7.4×10⁻²⁸ m/kg. 1000 kg ring, R=1 m, ω=1000 rad/s (beyond steel hoop-stress limit): B_g ~ 10⁻²¹ s⁻¹; realistic rigs 10⁻²⁴–10⁻²². Test-mass accel ~10⁻¹⁹ m/s²; best detectors (Eöt-Wash ~10⁻¹⁵ m/s²) are ≥4–6 orders short.
- Tajmar claim (gr-qc/0603033): ~10⁻⁴ g near spun-up Nb ring = 17–18 orders above GR. Canterbury ring laser (Graham et al., Physica C 468, 383 (2008)): null, ≥22× below Tajmar's prediction. Later signals attributed to instrument/acoustic artifacts. No independent confirmation; GR (~10⁻³¹ rad/s scale) stands.

## Negative mass
- Bondi (Rev. Mod. Phys. 29, 423, 1957): negative mass GR-consistent but (+,−) pair self-accelerates (runaway) conserving total E=p=0 (exact GR solution exists). EP → negative test mass still falls DOWN.
- Positive-energy theorems (Schoen–Yau 1979/81, Witten 1981): negative ADM mass forbidden for matter obeying dominant energy condition.
- Forward (J. Propulsion 6, 28, 1990): negative-matter propulsion bookkeeping.
- Khamehchi et al. (PRL 118, 155301, 2017): spin-orbit-coupled ⁸⁷Rb BEC — negative EFFECTIVE mass (dispersion curvature m* = ħ²(d²E/dk²)⁻¹ < 0), semiconductor-band analog; stress-energy positive; zero gravitational relevance.
- Farnes 2018 negative-mass dark fluid (A&A 620, A92): refuted (Socas-Navarro A&A 626, A5: wrong halos, cosmological runaway → near-c galaxy velocities; creation tensor = hidden Λ).
