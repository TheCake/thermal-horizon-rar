# Dossier: shielding limits, antimatter, and SC-gravity theory background (research agent output, 2026-07-20)

## Shielding
- Theory: no negative gravitational charge → no screening; positive energy theorem (Schoen–Yau 1979/81, Witten 1981: ADM mass ≥ 0 under DEC); static spin-2 exchange universally attractive; absorption would violate EP (tested ~10⁻¹³ Eöt-Wash, 10⁻¹⁵ MICROSCOPE).
- Majorana (1920) claimed absorption h ≈ 6.7×10⁻¹³ m²/kg. Modern limits: Zürich lab h < 4.3×10⁻¹⁵ (Unnikrishnan & Gillies, PRD 61, 101101R); 1997 Mohe eclipse gravimetry h < 6×10⁻¹⁹ (Yang & Wang 2002; eclipse "anomaly" doesn't fit shielding — UMG, PRD 63, 062002); best: **lunar laser ranging h < 10⁻²² m²/kg** (Eckhardt, PRD 42, 2144; Williams et al. 2009: (3±5)×10⁻²²) — 9 orders below Majorana. Allais pendulum effects → conventional atmospheric/thermal causes.

## ALPHA-g detail
- Nature 621, 716 (2023): a_g = 0.75 ± 0.13 ± 0.16 g downward; repulsive −1g excluded at p < 10⁻¹⁵; a=0 disfavored at 3.6σ. 2025: 15,000-atom trapping (Nat. Commun.) → path to 1%.

## Theory background
- DeWitt, PRL 16, 1092 (1966): Meissner effect expels the combined field B + (m/e)·(gravitomagnetic potential); flux of the combined field is quantized — founding paper.
- Ross, J. Phys. A 16, 1331 (1983): gravitational London equations. **Gravitational penetration depth λ_g = √(c²/4πGmn_s) ≈ 10¹³–10¹⁴ m (~0.1 light-year)**; λ_g/λ_e ≈ 10²¹. A lab SC expels gravitomagnetic flux to fractional depth (L/λ_g)² ~ 10⁻³⁰ — gravitational Meissner effect real in principle, nil in practice. Identity: (λ_e/λ_g)² = Gm²/(e²/4πε₀).
- Tajmar–de Matos conjectured B_g = 1.85×10⁻⁵ ω vs classical 3.19×10⁻³⁵ ω → enhancement ~6×10²⁹ ("field of a white dwarf").
- Ummarino & Gallerati (EPJC 77, 549, 2017): TDGL + gravito-Maxwell; max fractional local-g alteration **2.8×10⁻⁷ (YBCO at 87 K, near Tc)**, transient ~ns; Pb 10⁻¹⁷–10⁻¹⁵; their own hedge: "if it exists." Critique of formalism: Inan arXiv:1709.04352. This is the largest surviving peer-reviewed enhancement prediction — untested.
- Chiao SC gravitational-wave transducer: Berkeley 2003 null (conversion efficiency < 1.6×10⁻⁵); Kiefer & Weber (Ann. Phys. 14, 253, 2005): coupling "questionable."
- Quach gravitational Casimir (PRL 114, 081104, 2015): erratum PRL 118, 139901 (2017) — the Heisenberg–Coulomb graviton-reflection enhancement invalid; effect unmeasurably small without it.
- NASA BPP program (1996–2002, Millis 2005): 14 tasks, 6 null, 4 unresolved, 4 sequels; "no such breakthroughs appear imminent."
