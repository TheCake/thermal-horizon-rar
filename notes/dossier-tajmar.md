# Dossier: Tajmar experiments, full arc (research agent output, 2026-07-20; primary-source verified)

- Trigger: Tate et al. (PRL 62, 845, 1989) Cooper-pair mass anomaly in Nb: m*/2mₑ = 1.000084(21) measured vs 0.999992 predicted (Δm/m ≈ 9.2×10⁻⁵) — a real, published, still-not-fully-resolved precision anomaly.
- Tajmar & de Matos (Physica C 385, 551, 2003; 420, 56, 2005): to explain Tate, a gravitomagnetic field B_g = 2(Δm/m*)ω ≈ 1.84×10⁻⁴ ω would be needed — vs Earth's natural B_g ~ 10⁻¹⁴ rad/s.
- 2006 claim (gr-qc/0603033, ~250 runs, never journal-published): spinning Nb ring, angular accel ~1500 rad/s² → tangential ~100 µg; coupling −9.64±0.28×10⁻⁸ s² (Nb), Pb ≈ 84% of Nb, YBCO/BSCCO null. Their own words: "30 orders of magnitude higher than what general relativity predicts."
- 2007 gyro follow-up (arXiv:0707.3806): B_g/ω ~ 3–5×10⁻⁸ CW, ~10× smaller and sign-flipped CCW (parity anomaly), and **non-superconducting aluminum showed the signal too**; effect didn't decay with distance; onset not at Tc. γ ≈ 1.2–1.7×10¹⁸ over GR. Data ruled out their own Cooper-pair hypothesis by 5 orders.
- Canterbury UG-2 ring laser (Graham et al., Physica C 468, 383, 2008): 833.7 m² ring laser, Pb rotor at 4.2 K → measured slope (2.3±1.4)×10⁻⁶ cycles/rad, consistent with zero; any effect ≥21× (published: 22×) smaller than the Tajmar–de Matos prediction, ≥95% detection probability had it existed.
- Tajmar 2011 (SuST 24, 125011): military-grade FOG outside cryostat, 5000 RPM (50× earlier): **null within ±5×10⁻⁸ rad/s**; coupling bounds 4×10⁻¹¹ (SC), 3×10⁻¹⁰ (superfluid He) — earlier signals "most probably noise and data analysis artifacts"; named artifact: acoustic noise from liquid-helium expansion during rotation + MEMS vibration rectification. Improves on his 2006 claim by ~7 orders.
- SpaceDrive (TU Dresden): EmDrive µN signals traced to unshielded cables × Earth's magnetic field and thermal CoG drift (1 g CoG shift mimics 150 µN); final: all thrusts < 10–30 nN at 11 W → White et al. refuted by ~3 orders, Shawyer by ~5. Mach-effect thruster: vibration artifact near 20–50 kHz actuator resonance; noise < 5 nN.
