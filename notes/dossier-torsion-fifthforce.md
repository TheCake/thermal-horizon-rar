# Dossier: torsion, screened scalars, fifth-force walls (research agent output, 2026-07-20)

## Einstein–Cartan torsion
- Torsion is algebraic (doesn't propagate); vanishes outside spinning matter; vacuum ECSK = GR exactly (Hehl et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 48, 393, 1976; Trautman gr-qc/0606062).
- Leaves a four-fermion axial-axial contact term ∝ Għ²/c⁴ × (spin density)². Critical density where it matters: ~10⁵⁷ kg/m³ (nucleons) — ~40 orders beyond nuclear density.
- Eöt-Wash spin pendulum (10²³ polarized electron spins, PRD 78, 092006): anomalous spin couplings bounded at 10⁻²¹–10⁻²² eV; electron gravitational mass spin-direction-independent to 1 part in 10²¹. Spin-spin forces (PRL 111, 151802, 2013): axial coupling ≤ 3.8×10⁻⁴⁰ — ~37 orders below EM.

## Chameleon/symmetron scalars
- The force is ATTRACTIVE between ordinary bodies — cannot shield or repel even in principle.
- Atom interferometry (Hamilton 2015, Jaffe 2017, Sabulsky 2019) + levitated sensors (Yin 2022): canonical n=1 dark-energy chameleon essentially excluded end-to-end.

## The generic walls
- Inverse-square law: any gravitational-strength (|α|=1) Yukawa must have range λ < 38.6 µm (Lee et al., PRL 124, 101101, 2020).
- Equivalence principle: MICROSCOPE final η = (−1.5±2.7)×10⁻¹⁵ (PRL 129, 121102, 2022).
- **Quantum test masses obey the EP**: dual-isotope ⁸⁵Rb/⁸⁷Rb atom interferometry η = (1.6±5.2)×10⁻¹² (Asenbaum et al., PRL 125, 191101, 2020) — matter waves in superposition fall normally. The "coherent matter loophole" survives only for macroscopic condensate states (superconductor bulk), not for quantum coherence per se.
- Rothman & Boughn (Found. Phys. 36, 1801): graviton cross-section ~10⁻⁶⁸ cm²; a cm film reflecting gravitational waves (Minter–Chiao conjecture behind Quach's Casimir claim) would exceed matter's known coupling to gravitational radiation by ~40 orders.
