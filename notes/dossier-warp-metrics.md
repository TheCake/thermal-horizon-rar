- Pfenning & Ford 1997: QIs force wall ≲ 10² v_b Planck lengths; 100-m bubble at v=c needs ~ −6.2×10⁶² kg (≈10 orders beyond visible-universe mass).

- White/Eagleworks: 2021 EPJC paper = numerical Casimir energy map "qualitatively" resembling an Alcubierre slice; no bubble, no solved metric; media "first warp bubble" claims false. EmDrive refuted ≥3 orders (thermal artifact).
- Bobrick & Martire 2021: every warp drive is an inertially moving material shell; CANNOT self-accelerate; superluminal ⇒ negative energy.
- Lentz 2021 / Fell-Heisenberg 2021 "positive energy" claims REBUTTED by Santiago-Schuster-Visser (PRD 105, 064038): they only checked Eulerian observers; generic warp metrics violate NEC — even as pure geometry (null convergence condition), so modified gravity doesn't escape.

- Clough, Dietrich, Khan 2024: warp-bubble collapse waveforms ~300 kHz, h~10⁻²¹ at 1 Mpc; "no known equation of state" holds a bubble stable.
