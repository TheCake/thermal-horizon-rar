# Dossier: Podkletnov claims, replications, and Ning Li (research agent output, 2026-07-20)

## Podkletnov claims
- Podkletnov & Nieminen, Physica C 203, 441 (1992): 145 mm YBCO disc, levitated + rotated; claimed 0.05–0.3% weight loss above disc.
- 1996/97 (cond-mat/9701074, withdrawn from J. Phys. D after press exposure): 275 mm bilayer disc, ~5000 RPM + RF; claimed up to ~2%.

## Replications — all null
- NASA MSFC static (Li, Noever, Robertson, Koczor, Brantley, Physica C 281, 260, 1997): levitated bulk YBCO, gravimeter above → **< 2×10⁻⁸ g** change. ~4+ orders below Podkletnov's stationary claim (~5×10⁻⁴).
- NASA AC-motor rotating-field tests (NTRS 19990019627): 15 cm discs, rotating field to 12,000 RPM → null.
- NASA/SCI contract (~$600k, 1999–2002): 12-inch bilayer disc fabrication to replicate full setup; ended ~2002 with **no published measurement**.
- Hathaway, Cleveland & Bao, Physica C 385, 488 (2003): 160 mm bilayer disc, ≤550 RPM, RF → **null at 1×10⁻⁵** (0.001%).
- Woods et al., AIAA 2001-3363 (Sheffield, Project-Greenglow milieu): 50–75 mm discs, static + rotating; Podkletnov advised in 2000; conditions "never achieved" → null at balance sensitivity.
- Published critiques: de Podesta & Bull, Physica C 253, 199 (1995) — the 0.05–0.3% signal matches **buoyancy/convection artifacts** of the cold N₂ vapor around the hanging sample. Unnikrishnan, Physica C 266, 133 (1996) — a height-independent cylindrical "gravity shadow" is inconsistent with any field-source interpretation.
- Nobody ever replicated the full claimed configuration (275 mm levitated bilayer disc at 5000 RPM + RF). Boeing GRASP was an internal proposal Boeing declined to fund; BAE Greenglow (1996–2005) found nothing.

## Ning Li
- Li & Torr: PRD 43, 457 (1991); PRB 46, 5489 (1992); Torr & Li, Found. Phys. Lett. 6, 371 (1993). Mechanism: coherent lattice-ion rotation in a SC under applied AC/magnetic field → aligned ion gravitomagnetic moments add → macroscopic B_g. Secondary reviews report claims of ~10⁶ × Earth's natural gravitomagnetic field per tesla applied. (The pop-culture "10¹¹ enhancement" number could not be verified in primary text.)
- Refutations: **Kowitt, PRB 49, 704 (1994)** — Li–Torr conflated the phenomenological Meissner μ→0 with a microscopic permeability multiplying the gravitomagnetic response; magnitude "grossly overestimated." **Harris, Found. Phys. Lett. 12, 201 (1999)** — with realistic assumptions the induced fields are ~20 orders of magnitude smaller than claimed.
- Li herself co-authored the null NASA static test (Physica C 281, 1997). Left UAH ~1999, founded AC Gravity LLC; $448,970 DoD grant (2001–02); **no results ever published**. Last trace: 2003 MITRE talk. 2014 pedestrian accident → dementia; died July 27, 2021, Huntsville.
