### B.8 ELI12 capsule

Being heavy is the price of re-knitting your connection to the
universe every time you change your motion. The universe is slightly
warm, and warmth makes the knitting looser. Fast things pay full
price — that's Newton. Very slow things — the outskirts of galaxies,
stars in the widest pairs — get part of the price paid by the
universe's warmth, so moving them is easier than it should be, and it
LOOKS like extra gravity is pulling them. Dark matter is the receipt
we wrote for a discount we didn't know we were getting. And the ½
that keeps showing up in our fits is the half of the bill that empty
space itself always pays, warm or not.
