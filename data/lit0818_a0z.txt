   PRIMARY QUANTITY: a1_lock = +0.52 e-10 m/s^2 per unit z

    the point derivative at z = 0 is +0.49 e-10 and the two bracket the

(a) LOCK SLOPE vs THEIR PURE-SAMPLE BAND  a1 = -1.60 +/- 2.33
    1-sigma band : [-3.93, +0.73]   2-sigma band : [-6.26, +3.06]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(c) THE ZERO-POINT-OFFSET READING (their numbers only)
    anchored slope x window   : +5.23 x 0.09 = +0.4707 e-10
    their own intercept gap   : 1.54 - 1.15  = +0.3900 e-10
    residual                  : +0.0807 e-10 (21% of the gap)
    a slope reproducing the intercept gap alone across the window:
      +0.3900 / 0.09 = +4.33 e-10 per unit z
    the anchored a1 sits +0.85 sigma from that pure-offset slope
    VERDICT: the rise the anchored fit implies across its own redshift range
    is accounted for, to within 21% of the gap, by the offset between the
    two intercepts the same paper reports; an inter-sample zero-point
    difference and a slope are not separated by this lever arm.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUPPLEMENTARY (intercepts; systematics-dominated, reported for completeness)
    a0_lock(0) = 1.08 e-10
    their sample-alone intercept   1.54 +/- 0.11 -> +4.16 sigma from the lock
    their SPARC-anchored intercept 1.15 +/- 0.02 -> +3.38 sigma from the lock
    the gap between their OWN two intercepts (0.39 e-10) exceeds the distance
    from the lock to the anchored intercept (0.07 e-10) by a factor 5.8,
    which is the size of the inter-sample calibration unknown they flag.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CAVEATS ON THE COMPARISON
 1. The lock curve is evaluated at the stated cosmology above. Their fits
    adopt their own cosmology; over 0 < z < 0.09 the choice moves the lock
    slope far less than the quoted uncertainties on either of their a1 rows.
 2. Their a1 is the coefficient of a linear form; the lock is not linear.
    The secant and the point derivative differ by 0.034 e-10, which is the
    linearization ambiguity and is negligible against their error bars.
 3. No fit-direction or selection correction is applied here. Their own text
    flags fit direction, selection effects, and the constant mass-to-light
    assumption in the anchor sample as the limiting systematics.
