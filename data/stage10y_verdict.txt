Y-EXCLUDED-ANGLE-FAVOURED (BRANCH B4; SURVIVE-AXIS CONSTRUCTION-UNSTABLE
AND UNDER-POWERED)

  The registered grammar returns branch B4, and that return is correct and
  construction-robust: neither disagreement-cell signature fires on the
  registered between-galaxy error route, so no reachable value of the
  y-matched contrast could have produced B2 or B3. But "unresolved" is not
  what the stage measured. Read as continuous quantities against the
  stage's own pre-registered synthetic worlds -- all calibrated to
  reproduce the published -0.0857 contrast exactly, so the plain contrast
  itself carries no information -- the candidates separate cleanly on the
  disagreement cell. The cell reads -0.0177 +/- 0.0130 over 55 galaxies,
  against predictions of -0.0699 for a pure acceleration-composition
  world, +0.0000 for a pure angular world, and -0.1133 for a disk-scale
  world calibrated the same way: 4.0, 1.4 and 7.4 sigma. Restricted to the
  sixteen galaxies that carry inner points (with the world predictions
  computed on the same subsample), the comparison reads 1.8, 1.5 and 3.2
  sigma. The composition reading is excluded a second time on the
  y-matched contrast itself: -0.0947 with a leave-one-galaxy-out error of
  0.0493, a galaxy-block bootstrap that puts P(S1 > 0) at 0.009-0.010, and
  a matcher measured to remove a linear log-acceleration trend exactly
  (noise-free leak -0.00006) and to stay unbiased at half, double and
  curved trend strength. The three pooled detrend co-reads keep -0.063 to
  -0.084.

  The depression itself is real and aligned with the inner radii: the
  inner-cell delta is -0.0893 +/- 0.0349, negative in fourteen of nineteen
  galaxies (sign test p = 0.032), and a circular-shift null that preserves
  each curve's own autocorrelation places it beyond all 2000 registered
  draws (p <= 0.0005, the 1/2001 floor; beyond all 20 000 in the review's
  re-run; -5.2 shift-null sigma). That discharges the autocorrelation
  caveat this successor was registered to break, and the composition
  caveat falls in the same pass. The third caveat is NOT broken: the
  across-galaxy predictions of beam smearing are null (innermost-angle
  rho = +0.019, p = 0.94; distance rho = -0.318, p = 0.18, right sign, not
  significant), so the depression is organized BY angle without the
  between-galaxy gradient smearing would also produce. Angle is the
  organizer; smearing is not established as the mechanism.

  What stays open is the size of the non-angular admixture: on the Oi axis
  the two-world mixtures allow a composition fraction 0.254 +/- 0.186 and
  a disk-scale fraction 0.157 +/- 0.115, and the same circular-shift null
  puts the small residual Oi depression at p = 0.017-0.019. A fifteen to
  twenty-five per cent component is permitted and nominally present.

  Two instrument facts bound all of this. The survive/gap distinction is
  not a measurement: across matching windows 0.10-0.50 dex and window
  minima 3-10, SIXTEEN of eighteen constructions cross the two-error bar
  and none collapses (value family -0.079..-0.110, ratio family
  -1.69..-2.76 [GB SE-convention band -1.66..-2.75]), and the registered
  construction carries the largest error in the family. And the registered
  synthetic worlds under-disperse the S1 statistic by 1.4-1.8x relative to
  the sky's own realized scatter (world-realized SD 0.0268-0.0272
  registered / 0.0251-0.0257 sky-matched noise, vs sky 0.0493 jackknife /
  0.0385-0.0388 block bootstrap), so the gate-printed 0.968 is
  registered-world power, not sky power: at the measured error a true
  angular world of the published amplitude clears the survive bar only
  ~0.30 (jackknife bar) to ~0.62 (bootstrap bar) of the time -- B4 on the
  survive axis was the modal outcome even under a true angular reading.

  Nothing here moves the meter. H0 = 65.4 +/- 5.0 (stat) +/- 0.5, the form
  band 65.4-70.4 and the validity domain 62-75 are untouched: the stage
  runs no fit off the published parent optima and computes no Hubble
  constant (verified line-by-line by the round). Flow-leg statements carry
  the R48-C3 0.71-sigma baseline grade; the flow leg is null on every axis.
